Why This Matters

I was abandoned at birth. Raised in an orphanage. But even there, I faced abuse. At age 7, I was diagnosed with a rare disease. Still, I survived — alone. Today, I fight to protect children like I once was, by building safe spaces in the community.

Our Mission

CHANGE STAND is a nonprofit initiative to create community-based safe zones for children in Japan. We are building spaces where kids can eat, talk, and simply feel safe. Your support makes it possible.

Make an Impact

Your donation supports:

  • Facility rent and safety equipment
  • Food and education support
  • Outreach and advocacy for abused children
